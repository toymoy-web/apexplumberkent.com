Safety First

Before We Arrive

Find Your Shutoff

Shut off the main water valve if you have an active leak. In an apartment or commercial space, the shutoff may be controlled by building maintenance, so call them while you call us.

Kill Power To Wet Areas

Don't walk through standing water near outlets or a panel. Shut off power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

Clear The Immediate Area

Move belongings and equipment away from the water, and keep people out of any area where water is near electrical outlets or ceiling fixtures.

Local Knowledge

Southcenter's Commercial Core And Tukwila's Older Neighborhoods

Tukwila is the city directly north of Kent, with about 21,800 residents at the 2020 census. The Green River and Interstate 5 run through it from south to north, and that corridor shapes most of what happens here. A dense commercial center around Westfield Southcenter, one of the largest shopping centers in the Pacific Northwest, sits beside older residential neighborhoods on the slopes and flats around it.

Because of Southcenter, much of Tukwila's plumbing work looks different from what we see in Kent's hillside neighborhoods. Restaurants and food courts mean grease traps and floor drains. Big-box stores and hotels mean large water heaters and backflow assemblies. Tenant spaces change hands often enough that the plumbing behind the walls gets reworked more than once. We handle these as commercial calls and schedule around business hours when the situation allows.

The residential side is just as real. Foster, Allentown, Riverton, and Cascade View are older neighborhoods with a mix of single-family homes and apartment buildings. The older single-family streets are where we see aging supply lines, clay sewer laterals, and water heaters that have outlived their warranties. In multi-unit buildings, a leak in one unit often becomes a problem for the units below it, so a fast shutoff and a quick call matter more than usual.

Our commercial plumbing and drain technicians dispatch to every address inside ZIPs 98188 and 98168, from the Southcenter retail corridor to the older residential streets near the Green River Trail.

Southcenter Commercial Core

Restaurants, retail, hotels, and tenant spaces create steady demand for commercial plumbing, grease trap service, and emergency repairs during business hours and after.

Older Residential Neighborhoods

Foster, Allentown, Riverton, and Cascade View include older single-family homes where aging supply lines and sewer laterals are common calls.

Multi-Unit Buildings

Apartment and townhome buildings mean shared plumbing and neighbors below, so one leak can affect several units quickly.

Green River Floodplain

Parts of the city lie along the Green River, so groundwater and drainage matter for lower-lying properties.
