Safety First

Before We Arrive

Test Your Sump Pump Before Storm Season

Pour water into the basin to confirm the float switch triggers the pump. Do this before the first heavy rain, not during it.

Stay Out Of Standing Basement Water

Don't wade through water near outlets or a plugged-in pump. Shut off power to the area at the breaker if you can do so safely.

Know Your Main Shutoff Location

In a larger home, the shutoff can be less obvious. Find it now so you're not searching during an active leak.

Move Stored Items Off Basement Floors

Keep boxes and belongings off the floor in any basement or daylight lower level prone to moisture.

Local Knowledge

1980s-2000s Construction: What It Means For Your Plumbing

Maple Valley was settled in 1879 around coal and timber industries, but the city as it exists today, incorporated in 1997, grew almost entirely during the 1980s through early 2000s building boom. Homes here tend to be larger than Kent's own housing stock, 4 to 5 bedrooms is typical, on wooded lots with more square footage of supply and drain line per house than a comparable Kent home.

That construction era means most Maple Valley homes are on their original or first-replacement water heater by now, and a fair number back up to Lake Wilderness or sit on lots with real drainage considerations. Basements and daylight lower levels are common in this housing stock, which is exactly where a failed sump pump becomes a real problem rather than a minor inconvenience.

The Four Corners commercial district and the surrounding residential streets both largely date to this same building era. That means a consistent plumbing profile across most of the city, rather than the neighborhood-by-neighborhood variation we see in some of our other coverage areas.

Our sump pump and water heater technicians dispatch to every address inside ZIP 98038, from homes along Lake Wilderness to properties near the Four Corners commercial district.
