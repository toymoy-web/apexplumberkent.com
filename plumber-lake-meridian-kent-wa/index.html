Safety First

If Water Is Running Or Leaking

Find Your Shutoff

Shut off the main water valve if you have an active leak. It is usually near where the water line enters the house or at the meter box.

Keep Away From Standing Water Near Power

Don't walk through standing water near outlets or a panel. Shut off power to that area at the breaker only if it is safe to reach.

Clear The Area

Move belongings away from the water and keep people out of any area where water is near electrical outlets or ceiling fixtures.

Local Knowledge

About The Lake Meridian Area

Lake Meridian is an urban lake on Kent's East Hill, just off Kent-Kangley Road and surrounded by homes. The City of Kent's neighborhood council map lists an active Lake Meridian council and a separate Lake Meridian Ridge council, which shows the name is in real local use.

Lake Meridian Park sits on the lake, and a Wikipedia entry lists a swimming beach, fishing, a boat launch, a covered shelter, a playfield and restrooms, though that detail is flagged there as needing a citation. A 2006 Seattle Times profile of East Hill gave the park's address as 14800 Southeast 272nd Street. The same entry says it is one of the few lakes in King County that allow motorized boating.

The lake is fed mainly by springs at its west end and drains at the east end into a small stream that connects to Little Soos Creek and then the Green River. Lake Meridian is part of East Hill, so it overlaps our East Hill page. This page focuses on the homes and streets near the lake and the park.

Housing And Access

Homes Around The Lake

Lakeside And Nearby Homes

The 2006 East Hill profile mentioned lakefront estates along with more modest homes. We look at the real plumbing in each house and do not generalize.

Drainage And Sump Pumps

Where a house has a sump pit, the pump matters in wet weather. We do not assume that any property near the lake has flooding or high groundwater.

Outdoor Water Use

Spigots, irrigation and hose connections get more use near the water in summer. Check them for drips in spring, and shut them off if you see a leak.

Park And Event Days

Visitor traffic is heavier around the park in summer, so give us your street address and any access details when you call.

Helpful Notes

Practical Notes For Lake Area Homes

If your home has a sump pump, test it before heavy rain by lifting the float or pouring water into the pit. A pump that does not start needs attention before it is needed.

Check outdoor spigots and irrigation lines for drips each spring. A small leak outside can run for a long time unnoticed and raise your water bill.

A toilet that runs, a faucet that drips or a water heater that hisses is easy to fix early. Waiting turns a minor repair into water damage.
