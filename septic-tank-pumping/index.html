Overview

Septic Service In The Kent Area

Most of Kent proper is connected to city sewer, but properties on the surrounding unincorporated edges of King County are more likely to run on a private septic system. A septic tank needs periodic pumping, typically every three to five years depending on household size and tank capacity, to remove solid waste before it overflows into the drain field.

Skipping regular pumping is the most common reason a septic system fails early, since solids that reach the drain field can clog it in a way that's far more expensive to fix than routine pumping ever costs.

Signs You Need This

Signs Your Septic Tank Needs Pumping

Slow Drains Throughout The House

When every drain in the house is slow at once, rather than just one fixture, a full septic tank is a likely cause.

Sewage Odor Near The Tank Or Drain Field

A persistent smell outside near the septic system usually means it needs attention.

Standing Water Over The Drain Field

Soggy or pooling ground over the drain field area can mean the system is overloaded.

It's Been Over 3-5 Years

If you can't recall the last pumping, that's reason enough to schedule one before a backup forces the issue.

How We Fix It

Pumping And Inspection

We pump the tank completely, removing accumulated solids before they reach the drain field. While the tank is open, we also check the general condition of the tank and baffles, since catching a developing problem early is far cheaper than a full system failure later.

For a household on septic that's never had it serviced, we can help establish a realistic pumping schedule based on your tank size and household size, rather than guessing.

The drain field is just as important as the tank itself, and it's easy to damage without realizing it. Parking vehicles over it, planting trees with invasive root systems nearby, or directing downspout water onto it can all shorten a drain field's working life well before the tank itself would otherwise need attention.

FAQs

Common Questions

How often should a septic tank be pumped?

Typically every three to five years, depending on your household size and tank capacity. We can help establish a schedule if you don't have one.

Is my Kent property on septic or city sewer?

Most of Kent proper is on city sewer, but properties on the unincorporated edges of the area are more likely to be on a private septic system. We can help confirm which you have.

What happens if I skip pumping?

Solids eventually reach the drain field, which can clog it in a way that's far more expensive to repair than routine pumping.

Do you also handle septic backups?

Yes, we respond to active backups in addition to scheduled maintenance pumping.

Can I use regular household products with a septic system?

Harsh chemical drain cleaners and excessive bleach can disrupt the bacterial balance a septic tank relies on to break down waste, so it's worth being mindful of what goes down the drain.

How do I find my septic tank's access point?

It's usually marked by a small riser or cover near the tank's location, and if you're not sure where that is, we can help locate it during a service visit.
