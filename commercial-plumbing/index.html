Overview

Plumbing For Kent Businesses

Commercial plumbing covers everything from a single clogged fixture at a retail space to ongoing maintenance for a restaurant's grease trap, property-wide repairs for a management company, or annual backflow testing required for compliance. The scope and urgency look different from a typical home call, and we schedule around that.

Property managers and business owners in Kent call us for the same reasons homeowners do, a leak, a clog, a failed water heater, but with the added pressure of keeping a space operational and customers or tenants depending on it. We prioritize a fast diagnosis and a clear timeline so you know exactly what to expect before work starts and can plan around it.

Kent Context

Commercial Work In The Kent Valley

Kent's valley floor is a major industrial and warehouse district crossed by two main freight rail lines, and downtown around Kent Station and the accesso ShoWare Center adds restaurants, offices, and event venues. We handle backflow, grease traps, water heaters, and emergency repairs for these buildings and schedule around business hours.

Signs You Need This

Common Commercial Plumbing Needs

Recurring Grease Trap Issues

A restaurant or food service grease trap needs regular pumping and cleaning to avoid backups and code violations.

Required Backflow Testing

Many commercial properties and irrigation systems require annual backflow prevention testing to stay compliant.

Multi-Unit Or Property-Wide Leaks

A leak affecting multiple units or tenants needs a fast response to limit damage and downtime.

Fixture Or Line Repairs During Off-Hours

Retail and office spaces often need work scheduled outside business hours to avoid disrupting operations.

How We Fix It

How We Work With Businesses And Property Managers

We start with a clear scope and timeline before any work begins, since a business needs to plan around downtime and staffing in a way a household typically doesn't. For property managers handling multiple units, we coordinate directly and provide documentation useful for records or tenant communication.

Backflow testing and grease trap service are scheduled proactively where possible, rather than only in response to a failure, since staying ahead of a compliance requirement is almost always cheaper than a violation or fine.

We also handle general facility plumbing for offices, retail spaces, and light industrial properties around Kent's commercial corridors, from restroom fixture repairs to water heater service for a break room or shared facility. Documentation of completed work is provided on request, which matters for property records and lease compliance for tenants who need to show maintenance history.

Commercial Calls

Commercial Leaks, Floor Drains, And Multi-Unit Water Problems

Commercial Leak Detection

A rising water bill, water surfacing in a parking lot, or a leak on a business property needs the source located before any digging. We isolate the system and trace the line.

Floor Drain Overflows

An overflowing floor drain or a drain grate that must come off right away can stop a business from operating. Call our 24/7 line and we will clear it and find the cause.

Apartment And Multi-Unit Water Problems

Several units on one building often means several possible sources. We isolate each riser and branch line, then report which one is the problem.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do you work with property management companies?

Yes, we regularly coordinate directly with property managers across multiple units or properties.

Can you test backflow preventers for compliance?

Yes, we provide annual backflow prevention testing for commercial properties and irrigation systems.

Do you service restaurant grease traps?

Yes, we handle both scheduled grease trap maintenance and response calls for backups.

Can work be scheduled after business hours?

Yes, we regularly schedule commercial work outside normal hours to avoid disrupting operations.

Do you provide invoices suitable for business accounting?

Yes, every job includes a detailed invoice with a breakdown of parts and labor, useful for expense tracking and property maintenance records.

Can you provide references for larger commercial jobs?

Yes, we're happy to discuss past commercial work relevant to your specific type of property when scoping a larger project.

What's included in a backflow testing visit?

We test the backflow preventer against the required standard, provide documentation for your records or the local water authority, and note any repairs needed to bring it into compliance.

Do you handle multi-tenant properties?

Yes, we regularly work with apartment complexes and mixed-use properties, coordinating scheduling with a property manager to minimize disruption to tenants during shared-system repairs or maintenance.
