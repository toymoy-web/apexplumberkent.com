Safety First

If Water Is Running Or Leaking

Find Your Shutoff

Shut off the main water valve if you have an active leak. It is usually near where the water line enters the house or at the meter box.

Keep Away From Standing Water Near Power

Don't walk through standing water near outlets or a panel. Shut off power to that area at the breaker only if it is safe to reach.

Clear The Area

Move belongings away from the water and keep people out of any area where water is near electrical outlets or ceiling fixtures.

Local Knowledge

Where West Hill Sits In Kent

West Hill is a commonly used name for Kent's western neighborhoods near Interstate 5. The City of Kent's 2011 Midway Subarea Plan describes the Midway area along Pacific Highway South, also State Route 99, as the commercial spine for Kent's West Hill residents. We do not claim an exact boundary for West Hill, because the city does not publish one as a single line.

Residents organize around the name. The City of Kent's neighborhood council map lists two active groups, Neighbors of West Hill and West Hill Cambridge. That tells us the name is in real local use and that these are established residential pockets inside the larger area.

West Hill is also where Kent meets its neighbors to the west. Des Moines and SeaTac both have their own pages on this site, and many West Hill addresses are a short drive from either. The Midway plan was written to prepare the corridor for future growth around transit, which is one reason this area mixes housing with commercial uses.

Helpful Notes

Practical Notes For Homes And Small Businesses

For apartments and townhomes, find the unit shutoff and tell your property manager about any leak right away. Water often travels through ceilings and walls to neighbors, so quick notice protects everyone below you.

For restaurants and shops, kitchen and restroom drains are the usual trouble spots. Pouring grease down a drain is a common cause of recurring clogs, and a commercial plumber can advise on what your fixtures need.

For houses, a water heater that leaks or a drain that backs up more than once deserves a proper look. Repeat problems are rarely fixed by plunging alone, and a camera or a drain cleaning can show what is actually in the line.
