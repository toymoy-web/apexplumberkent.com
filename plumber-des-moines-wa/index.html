Local Knowledge

Marina District, Redondo, And Zenith: A Neighborhood-By-Neighborhood Look

Des Moines earned its nickname, 'The Waterland City,' honestly, it's the only city between Seattle and Tacoma with direct public marina access to Puget Sound. The Marina District's homes are some of the city's oldest, dating back toward the town's 1867 founding and the decades around its 1959 incorporation, and older supply lines and drain systems are a real, recurring theme there.

Further from the water, the Redondo neighborhood near the Federal Way border and the Zenith and Woodmont areas near Saltwater State Park see a different mix, generally later construction with fewer of the original-pipe issues common closer to the marina. Across all of Des Moines, the proximity to Puget Sound means water damage and drainage calls carry a bit more urgency than they might further inland.

The area around Highline College adds a third layer to the picture, a mix of long-term homeowners and renters whose plumbing needs and urgency levels can differ meaningfully from the waterfront-adjacent neighborhoods just a short drive away.

Our water damage and drain technicians dispatch to every address across both Des Moines ZIP codes, 98148 and 98198, from the Marina District out to Redondo near the Federal Way border.

Marina District's Older Homes

Housing near the historic downtown marina area tends to be older, with a higher rate of original supply-line and drain issues.

Redondo & Zenith Newer Construction

Neighborhoods further from downtown, near the Federal Way border and Saltwater State Park, generally have newer plumbing systems.

Puget Sound Proximity

Being directly on the Sound means water damage and drainage concerns get taken seriously here, given the area's real exposure to moisture.

Highline College Area

The area near Highline College sees a mix of long-term residents and renters, with plumbing needs spanning both profiles.
