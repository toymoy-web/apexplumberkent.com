Safety First

If Water Is Running Or Leaking

Find Your Shutoff

Shut off the main water valve if you have an active leak. It is usually near where the water line enters the house or at the meter box.

Keep Away From Standing Water Near Power

Don't walk through standing water near outlets or a panel. Shut off power to that area at the breaker only if it is safe to reach.

Clear The Area

Move belongings away from the water and keep people out of any area where water is near electrical outlets or ceiling fixtures.

Local Knowledge

Where East Hill Sits In Kent

East Hill is the part of Kent on the hillside east of the Green River valley and downtown. A 2006 Seattle Times neighborhood profile noted that its boundaries depend on whom you ask. It said the City of Kent generally places East Hill between 94th Avenue South and Soos Creek, running roughly from Southeast 192nd to Southeast 284th Street. We use those anchor streets and do not claim an official boundary.

The same profile described East Hill as mainly residential with a stretch of businesses. It listed housing from apartments and condos to affordable single-family homes and lakefront estates, and reported that about 74 percent of housing units were owner-occupied at that time. That figure is from 2006, so treat it as background and not a current statistic.

Green space is part of daily life here. The profile listed East Hill Park, the East Hill Skate Park, Lake Meridian Park and Wilson Playfields run by the City of Kent, plus King County's North Meridian Park near Southeast 231st Street and 120th Avenue Southeast. Meridian Valley Country Club is a golf course community with its own homeowners association.

Helpful Notes

Practical Plumbing Notes For Hillside Homes

Learn where your main water shutoff is before you ever need it. It is usually near where the water line enters the house or at the meter box. A quick test each year to make sure it turns freely can save a lot of trouble if a pipe ever fails.

Water heaters have a limited working life, and age is the usual reason for a call. If yours is leaking at the tank, shut off the water and power or gas to it and call. A slow drain that returns after plunging usually means the blockage is deeper than the fixture.

Do not guess about older materials. General information says some older homes may have been repiped over time and some have not, and the only way to know is to look. A technician can check exposed pipes and recommend what is actually needed.

FAQs

Questions From East Hill Residents

Where does East Hill start and stop?

The City of Kent generally places East Hill between 94th Avenue South and Soos Creek, roughly from Southeast 192nd to Southeast 284th Street, according to a 2006 Seattle Times profile. Exact boundaries are not fixed, so call with your address if you are near an edge.

Do you serve homes near Lake Meridian?

Yes. Lake Meridian is on East Hill and has its own page here with more detail about the lake and the city park.

Who provides water and sewer on East Hill?

It depends on your address. Your water or sewer bill names your provider, and that provider can confirm where its service area begins and ends.

My condo has no water. Who should I call first?

Call your building manager or HOA first, because the shutoff or the problem may be a shared line. You can call us at the same time to talk through the issue.

Does my HOA affect plumbing work?

It can. Some associations control common lines or require notice for outside work. Check your association documents and tell us if shared plumbing may be involved.
