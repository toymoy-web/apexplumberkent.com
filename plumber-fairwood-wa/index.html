Safety First

Before We Arrive

Find Your Shutoff

Shut off the main water valve if you have an active leak. If you are unsure where it is, look near where the water line enters the house or at the meter box at the street.

Kill Power To Wet Areas

Don't walk through standing water near outlets or a panel. Shut off power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

Clear The Immediate Area

Move belongings and equipment away from the water, and keep people out of any area where water is near electrical outlets or ceiling fixtures.

Local Knowledge

An Unincorporated Plateau Community Beside Kent And Renton

Fairwood is a census-designated place in King County with 19,396 residents at the 2020 census. It is unincorporated, which means it is not part of any city. Voters rejected incorporation in 2006 and 2009 and turned down annexation to Renton in 2010.

The community sits on a plateau between the Cedar River bluffs and the Kent Valley, close to State Route 167, with Renton to the north and west. It shares ZIP code 98058 with Renton and the neighboring Maple Heights-Lake Desire area. That is why a Fairwood home can carry a Renton mailing address even though it is not inside the City of Renton.

Outdoor space is a big part of the area. Petrovitsky Park covers 88 acres, the Soos Creek Trail and Park follows a creek corridor, and Fairwood Golf and Country Club has an 18-hole course. Many homes sit on streets close to these green areas, where drainage and groundwater after heavy rain are worth a plumber's attention.

Because there is no city utility, water and sewer come from special districts. The Soos Creek Water and Sewer District serves Kent, Covington, Maple Valley, Renton and parts of unincorporated southeast King County, and other districts serve other addresses. Service areas follow addresses and not community names, so check your bill or ask the district.
