Safety First

Before We Arrive

Find Your Shutoff

Shut off the main water valve if you have an active leak. In an apartment or condo, the shutoff may be controlled by building maintenance, so call them while you call us.

Kill Power To Wet Areas

Don't walk through standing water near outlets or a panel. Shut off power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

Clear The Immediate Area

Move belongings and equipment away from the water, and keep people out of any area where water is near electrical outlets or ceiling fixtures.

Local Knowledge

Town Square, Olde Burien, And The Puget Sound Shoreline

Burien is a western King County city of about 52,000 residents, with 52,066 counted in the 2020 census. It incorporated on February 28, 1993 and has about 5.5 miles of Puget Sound shoreline. Seattle's White Center area and Boulevard Park lie to the north, Tukwila and SeaTac to the east, and Normandy Park to the south.

The city center is Town Square, finished in 2017, which combines shops, apartments, condos, a senior living center, a King County Library branch and City Hall on the library's top floor. Olde Burien, around SW 152nd Street on the west side of Ambaum Boulevard, is the older commercial strip. Three Tree Point sits just west of town on the water.

That mix shapes our calls. Apartments and condos near the center mean shared plumbing and neighbors below, so a fast shutoff matters. Older storefronts around Olde Burien mean commercial drains and water heaters. Homes closer to the shoreline are a mix of ages, and we check the actual pipes before recommending any repair.

Burien also has no single water or sewer utility. The City of Burien lists Highline Water District, King County Water Districts 20, 49 and 125, and Seattle Public Utilities for water, plus Midway, Southwest Suburban and Valley View for sewer. Which one serves you depends on your street, and the city publishes district maps so you can look it up.

Town Square And City Center
