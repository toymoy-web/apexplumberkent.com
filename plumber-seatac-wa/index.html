Safety First

Before We Arrive

Know Your Main Shutoff

Shut off the main water valve first if you have an active leak. In SeaTac's older homes, it's often in the crawlspace or basement rather than a garage.

Kill Power To Wet Areas

Don't walk through standing water near outlets or a panel. Shut off power to that specific area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

Clear The Immediate Area

Move anything valuable away from the leak, especially in an older home where a slow leak may have already reached nearby materials.

Local Knowledge

Why SeaTac's Older Housing Stock Means More Frequent Plumbing Calls

SeaTac wasn't incorporated as its own city until 1990, the youngest incorporation of any city in our coverage area, but the neighborhoods themselves are much older. Much of the housing in McMicken Heights and the surrounding area dates to the 1930s through 1950s, built during the wartime Boeing defense boom and the years around Sea-Tac Airport's 1949 opening. That makes SeaTac's plumbing genuinely older than what we see in Covington, Maple Valley, or even Kent's newer neighborhoods.

Angle Lake's waterfront homes add a second, different profile: private lake-adjacent properties with their own drainage and supply-line considerations. Across SeaTac generally, original galvanized supply lines and aging clay sewer laterals are common. A repiping consultation is one of our most requested services from this city specifically.

SeaTac's dense mix of long-term homeowners and airport-corridor rental housing also means we see a wider range of property conditions here than in some of our newer-construction coverage areas. That range runs from meticulously maintained original homes to units where deferred maintenance has caught up with the plumbing.

Our repiping and sewer line technicians dispatch to all five SeaTac ZIP codes, 98148, 98158, 98168, 98188, and 98198, from McMicken Heights out to the Angle Lake shoreline.
