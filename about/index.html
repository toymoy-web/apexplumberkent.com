Who We Are

Local Dispatch, Straightforward Pricing

Kent Apex Plumbing is a plumbing dispatch based in Kent, Washington, registered with the Washington State Department of Labor & Industries, the same requirement that applies to every legitimate plumbing contractor in King County. We dispatch technicians across Kent's East Hill, West Hill, and valley-floor neighborhoods, and into five nearby cities: Covington, Maple Valley, Des Moines, SeaTac, and Black Diamond.

We built this business around a simple standard: diagnose the actual problem, explain it in plain language, and give a written price before any work begins. That standard applies whether the call is a five-minute faucet repair or a full-day repipe.

Our Standards

What We Won't Do

We don't fabricate reviews, star ratings, or testimonials, and you genuinely won't find any on this site. We don't quote a low number to get in the door and raise it once the work is underway. And we don't recommend a bigger job than a home actually needs, whether that's a full repipe when a targeted repair would do, or a water softener a household's own water doesn't call for.

When a technician arrives, the first step is always diagnosis, not a sales pitch. We explain what we found, in plain language, and give you a written price before anything happens. If the honest answer is a smaller job than you expected, that's the answer you'll get, every single time.

Who We Work With

Homeowners And Businesses

Most of our calls come from homeowners, but we regularly work with property managers, landlords, and small businesses across our coverage area too. A rental property's plumbing gets the same upfront pricing and honest diagnosis as a single-family home, and we're happy to provide documentation for a property manager's records or a landlord's maintenance log when it's needed.

For commercial accounts, backflow testing and grease trap service are available on a scheduled basis, not just as a response to a failure. We'd rather help a business stay ahead of a compliance requirement than show up after it's already become a violation.

FAQs

Common Questions

How long has Kent Apex Plumbing been in business?

We're a newer dispatch built specifically to serve Kent and the surrounding Green River Valley, registered with the state to operate as required by Washington law for any plumbing contractor.

Are your technicians employees or subcontractors?

Our technicians are dispatched directly for every job, and each one is state-registered to perform plumbing work in Washington.

Do you carry insurance?

Yes. If you'd like documentation for a specific job, such as for an HOA or property manager, let us know when you call.

Can I request the same technician for a repeat visit?

We'll do our best to accommodate that request based on scheduling, though it isn't guaranteed for every call.

Do you subcontract any work to outside companies?

No, our dispatched technicians handle the plumbing work directly, from start to finish. We don't hand your job off to an unrelated third-party contractor.

What areas do you consider your core coverage?

Kent, plus Covington, Maple Valley, Des Moines, SeaTac, and Black Diamond. All six are on the same daily dispatch routes, with no distance surcharge between them.

Getting In Touch

Reach Us Directly

We don't use a contact form anywhere on this site; every inquiry goes through a real conversation, by phone, text, or email, with an actual person on the other end.
