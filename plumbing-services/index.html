Pricing

How We Price A Job

Every non-emergency job starts with a free written estimate before any work begins. For an emergency call, we still give you an upfront price before starting the actual work, though the diagnostic visit itself isn't free outside standard business hours. Either way, you'll know the cost before you commit, not after the fact.

Pricing depends on the specific job, not a flat rate sheet. A cartridge swap on a dripping faucet costs a fraction of a full repipe, and we'll never quote a bigger job than the diagnosis actually supports. If a smaller fix genuinely solves the problem, that's the option we'll recommend for your home.

FAQs

Questions About Our Services

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, on non-emergency work. For emergency calls, we give you an upfront price before any work begins, even though the estimate itself isn't free after-hours.

Which service do I need if I'm not sure what's wrong?

Call and describe the symptom, a slow drain, a high water bill, no hot water, and we'll point you to the right service, or just schedule the visit directly.

Do you offer emergency service for every listed service?

Most calls can be handled as emergencies. A few, like water softener installation or filtration setup, are typically scheduled rather than same-day emergency work.

Are all of these services available in every city you serve?

Yes, every service listed here is available across Kent and all five nearby cities we cover.

Do you offer any combined discounts for multiple services in one visit?

Sometimes, depending on the jobs involved. Ask when you call and we'll let you know if a combined visit makes sense for your situation.

What if my issue doesn't match any service listed here?

Call anyway and describe it. Most plumbing issues fall under one of these categories even if the exact wording doesn't match what you're searching for.
