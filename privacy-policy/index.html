Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026.

Information We Collect

When you call, text, email, or otherwise contact Kent Apex Plumbing, we may collect information you provide directly, including your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about the plumbing issue you're contacting us about.

Our website does not use contact forms. Information is collected only through direct communication, phone calls, texts, or emails, that you initiate with us.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to schedule and complete service calls, communicate about appointments and estimates, and maintain records of work performed at your property. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Information Sharing

We may share information with subcontractors or technicians dispatched to complete your service call, and with call-tracking or scheduling service providers we use to operate our business. We do not share your information for third-party marketing purposes.

Website Analytics

Our website may use standard analytics tools to understand general traffic patterns. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify individual visitors.

Contacting Us About This Policy

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, contact us at (253) 275-5116 or contact@apexplumberkent.com.
